Spinal Stenosis

Spinal stenosis is a narrowing of the spinal canal that puts pressure on the spinal cord and nerves, often in the neck or lower back.

Free Pain Evaluation

What it is

As the canal narrows — from arthritis, thickened ligaments or bone spurs — the nerves have less room, leading to pain, numbness and difficulty walking that often eases when leaning forward or sitting.

Common symptoms

  • Leg or arm pain when standing or walking
  • Numbness, tingling or heaviness
  • Relief when bending forward
  • Balance difficulty

How we treat it

We tailor treatment to your anatomy, from interventional pain management to minimally invasive decompression and, when needed, fusion to stabilize the spine.
